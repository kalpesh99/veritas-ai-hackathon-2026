    Small model. Clear limits.

    Find information, prepare a conversation.

    The model learns word importance from a small, authored catalog using TF-IDF, then ranks topics by cosine similarity. It runs entirely in your browser. It does not generate medical answers or estimate disease risk.

    What we tested — and what we have not

    Our repository contains a reproducible synthetic question benchmark and a fixed-topic baseline. This tests information retrieval only. It is not a patient study, medical accuracy measurement, or proof of safety. English words, a tiny catalog and unfamiliar phrasing can cause irrelevant matches. Always check the source and ask a health professional.

    Where the information comes from

    Educational summaries were written for this project using three NIDDK pages: Symptoms & causes, Tests & diagnosis, and Managing diabetes. They are not clinician-reviewed. Source links are attached to each topic.
