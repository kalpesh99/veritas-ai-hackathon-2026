    Broader language. Honest limits.

    Meaning-based suggestions, confirmed by you.

    A pretrained MiniLM sentence model compares your description with nine authored health-topic descriptions. Unlike the earlier exact-word demo, it can relate different ways of expressing a similar idea. It can also be wrong. It does not generate medical answers or assess symptoms. Similarity is used to offer possible reading, not decide what condition someone has.

    Sources, limits and privacy

    Topic cards link to MedlinePlus (U.S. National Library of Medicine), NIDDK and CDC. Summaries and follow-up prompts are authored, not clinician-reviewed. English only; limited topics; no triage, diagnosis or treatment. Topic selection is your choice. The browser downloads model assets but processes your text on your device. A technical failure leaves manual browsing and notes available. Printing, copying or downloading creates a copy you control.
