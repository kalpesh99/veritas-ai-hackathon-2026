OUR OWN QUESTION

What changes when labs go missing?

We randomly hid one or two additional lab values in the test set, repeated 20 times, and kept the same model and threshold.

These are stress-test averages, not new patients or clinical performance guarantees. Real-world missingness may behave differently.

The limits we would want you to know +

A small, selected sample

UCI’s original dataset contains 400 records collected over a short period. We use only records with known adult ages. Its CKD frequency does not represent the general population.

Current labels, not future risk

The model classifies the study’s CKD label using related laboratory measurements. It does not predict who will develop CKD, and strong test results can reflect a highly separable study sample.

Missingness can mislead

Unknown numbers use training medians; unknown categories use the training mode. This can hide important information. More than two missing fields, an absent adult age, or an out-of-training-range value blocks the score.

More validation comes first

Preprocessing is fitted on training data only. Identical input profiles stay together across splits. External validation, calibration and broader fairness testing remain unfinished.
